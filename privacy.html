Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

No Data Collection

AshiPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All content you create and edit stays entirely on your device.

Local Storage

AshiPad may use your browser's local storage to save your preferences (such as hiding the welcome modal). No document content is stored or transmitted.

No Third-Party Tracking

This application does not use any analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party services. Your activity is not monitored or recorded.

No External Communication

AshiPad does not send any data to external servers. There is no backend, no API, and no network communication of any kind.

Your Data

You have full control over your content. You can export, copy, or delete your documents at any time. No data leaves your browser.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in this page. Continued use of AshiPad constitutes acceptance of any updates.
